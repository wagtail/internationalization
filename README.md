# [Wagtail internationalization (i18n) team](https://wagtail.github.io/internationalization/)

_internationalization is sometimes abbreviated as "i18n [18 letters between i and n]"._

👋 Join [#internationalization](https://wagtailcms.slack.com/archives/C0CCBPD0NNB) in the [Wagtail Slack](https://github.com/wagtail/wagtail/wiki/slack).

This repository stores documentation and working documents relating to internationalization efforts in Wagtail.

## What we do

The internationalization team focuses on improving Wagtail’s support for multi-lingual sites, ensuring that content can be easily translated and managed across different languages. We maintain [wagtail-localize](https://github.com/wagtail/wagtail-localize/), Wagtail's first-party library that adds comprehensive content translation capabilities to the CMS.

We focus on high level aspects that make up _internationalization_ in Wagtail, such as content translation workflows, language management, and integration with third-party localization tools. On occasion, our work may touch on _localization_ topics as well. See [Localization vs. Internationalization](https://www.w3.org/International/questions/qa-i18n) for an explanation of the difference.

## Why not name yourself 'wagtail localize team' then?

The team chose the name "internationalization" to reflect its broader focus on all aspects of supporting multi-lingual sites in Wagtail, rather than being limited to the development and maintenance of the wagtail-localize library.
